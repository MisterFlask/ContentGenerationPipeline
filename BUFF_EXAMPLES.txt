
Summary

The game defines numerous buffs across different categories. Below are excerpts showing how buffs provide their effects.

Armored – start of turn gain Block equal to stacks

Burning – deals 4 damage plus Blood at turn end or on discard, then decreases stacks

Badass (persona trait) – at combat start the owner gains Mettle equal to stacks

Tougher Enemies (location) – enemies start combat with 40 % more max HP

Toxic Retaliation (enemy) – when struck, applies Poison equal to stacks to the attacker

The Tariffed buff illustrates a playable card buff that increases energy cost by its stacks

Buff list (cards/characters/locations)

Standard character buffs

Armored – gain Block each turn.

Bulwark – increased Block given by stacks.

Lethality – +1 damage per stack.

Temporary Lethality – same as Lethality but expires end of turn.

Weak – 33 % less damage dealt per stack, decreases each turn.

Vulnerable – 50 % more damage taken for N turns.

Intangible – every hit reduced to 1 damage, removed end of turn.

Frostbite – lose 1 Dexterity and Lethality per stack.

Titan – reduce incoming damage by stacks.

Regeneration – heal stacks at turn end.

Burning – 4+damage each turn/discard (scales with Blood).

Playable card buffs

Buster – +50 % damage against blocked enemies per stack.

Cost Increased – card costs +1 for N turns.

Tariffed – card costs stacks more energy.

Painful – when played, deal stacks damage to owner.

Sweeper – card damages all enemies for stacks when played.

RetainBuff – card stays in hand.

Ethereal – card exhausts at end of turn.

Volatile – on discard, card plays itself then exhausts another card.

Unstable – card exhausts if retained.

Haunting – if retained, owner gains Stress stacks.

Figment – card exhausts if in hand at end of turn.

Sticky – if retained, apply Stunned equal to stacks to owner.

Stunned – next stacks cards drawn cost +1.

Heavy – on draw, owner draws 1 fewer next turn.

Lightweight – on draw, draw an extra card up to stacks times.

Buster, Doubled, HatchesIntoEnemyIfRetained, etc. provide other effects as defined.

Persona buffs (permanent character traits)

Badass – start combat with Mettle.

Blood Knight – start combat with Blood.

Blinding Tattoo – start combat applying Weak to all enemies.

Botched Tattoo – start combat with Weak.

Capitalist Soul – start combat with Venture.

Courageous – start combat with Fearless.

Daring – start combat with Pluck.

Heavy Smoker – start combat with Smog.

Scholar – start combat with Ashes.

Undersider – gain Hell Currency at run start.

Prepper – chance for extra energy at combat start (percent equal to stacks).

Merchant – at run start, a cargo card sells for stacks more Hell currency.

Well-Drilled – revolver attacks deal stacks bonus damage.

Sadist – on kill, remove stacks Stress from owner.

Protective – bonus block to allies when blocking them.

Selfish – owner’s blocks are stacks higher on self, stacks lower on others.

Thief – merchants have one free card (logic stub).

Giant Killer – start combat with Buster stacks.

Strong Back – apply Lightweight to random cargo card at combat start.

Huntsman and SoulTrapper are placeholders.

Location buffs

Additional Hell Currency – adds a currency reward equal to stacks.

Additional Pounds Sterling – adds currency reward equal to stacks.

Another Card Reward – adds an extra card reward.

Upgraded Card Rewards – card rewards are upgraded.

Guaranteed Relic / Guaranteed Rare Card – ensures specified reward after combat.

Guaranteed Specific Relic – gives a specific relic at combat end.

Merchant Multiplier – merchant offers stacks % more for cargo.

Rock Slides – on location entry all allies take stacks damage.

Eldritch Foes – all enemies start combat with Eldritch Horror.

Tougher Enemies – enemies start with 40 % more max HP.

Treasure-debuff files (Windstorm, Trapped, etc.) are placeholders.

Monster-specific buffs
(all reside in src/gamecharacters/buffs/enemy_buffs)

Absolute Zero Doctrine – each turn the healthiest hero gains Frostbite and you gain a Warm Yourself card.

Accursed – players start combat with Cursed stacks.

Audit Pressure – if >3 cards played this turn, players lose Dexterity stacks.

Blood God – when a sacrifice occurs, enemy gains Lethality stacks.

Cargo Sensitive – playing a cargo card deals 20 damage to the buffed enemy.

Decaying – lose stacks HP at end of turn (ignores block).

Delicious – when struck, attacker gains Next Turn Lethality stacks.

Designated Foe – mark from Duelist; others deal no damage.

Desirous – first stacks cards each turn exhaust on play.

Despair Nexus – end of turn reduce each resource by stacks.

Do Not Look At Me – targeting this enemy applies Stress stacks to the attacker.

Duelist – marks a random player as Designated Foe; takes no damage from others.

Ear Worm – status card dealing stacks damage each turn; damage increases each turn kept.

Egg Layer – at turn start adds “Eggs” to random draw pile cards.

Fear Eater – damage bonus = stacks × target stress.

Foreseen – playing this card gives Lethality stacks to all enemies.

Grand Armée Eternal – when an ally dies, gain Lethality 2.

Greed Incarnate – cargo cards heal enemy and give Lethality; if it takes 50+ damage in one turn it grants 15 currency.

Guilt – playing cost>2 cards exhausts stacks cards from discard.

Harbinger of Fate – flavor buff, no additional effect.

Idol – when attacked, all enemy intents target the attacker.

Leeching Bite – heals stacks HP when dealing damage.

Lumbering – every card played adds stacks % damage taken for the turn.

Minion – dies when no non‑minion enemies remain.

Muse – playing a zero-cost card grants Lethality stacks.

Penance – playing cost ≤2 cards increases their cost by stacks.

Production Quota – whenever a card exhausts, deal 3 damage to all heroes.

Prophet – 20 % chance on drawing a card to apply Foreseen stacks.

Regeneration – heals stacks HP each turn.

Robotic – negates every other debuff.

Rust Monster – on hit, reduce block on a random card by stacks.

Self Destruct – after secondaryStacks turns deals 999 self-damage and stacks damage to all players.

Signal Interference – whenever a player card is played, deal stacks damage to a random player.

Tariff Aura – at start of turn a random hand card costs +1 energy.

Toxic Retaliation – when struck, apply Poison stacks to attacker.
